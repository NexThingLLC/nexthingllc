Common
Questions

What does NexThing do?

NexThing is an architect-led consulting firm that builds automation for real business problems. That includes AI agents where they pay off, workflow and process automation, and the infrastructure underneath: public cloud, hybrid cloud, Kubernetes, on-prem, and OpenStack, plus site reliability engineering and DevOps.

How much does an AI Opportunity Assessment cost?

The AI Opportunity Assessment is a fixed-price, two-week engagement for $2,500. It delivers a prioritized roadmap ranked by effort and impact, a recommendation for your highest-value first build, practical cost and effort estimates, and a working session to walk through the findings with your team.

Do I need AI, or just automation?

Often the answer is simpler automation. AI agents earn their place where a task needs judgment or language, such as reading documents, triaging requests, or answering questions from your own knowledge. Rules-based work is usually faster, cheaper, and more reliable with conventional automation. NexThing recommends whichever fits the problem.

Is my data safe?

Solutions are designed to run in your environment wherever possible, with access limited to what each task needs, human approval for high-stakes actions, and logging of what automated agents do. How your data is handled is agreed in writing before any work begins.

Do you work with on-prem, OpenStack, or hybrid cloud environments?

Yes. Not every workload belongs in the public cloud. NexThing designs, builds, and operates on-prem, OpenStack, and hybrid environments as well as AWS, Azure, and GCP, and builds automation that runs where your systems already live.

Where does NexThing work?

NexThing works with businesses across the United States. Engagements are delivered remotely.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute call. It is a straight conversation about your goals with the person who would actually do the work, and whether NexThing is the right fit.
